# Recursive organization example

System-in-focus: a product organization whose S1 units are a coding organization and a research organization.

```text
Product organization
├── Coding organization (parent-level S1)
│   ├── delivery operations
│   ├── coordination and current control
│   ├── independent operational checks
│   └── local intelligence and policy
└── Research organization (parent-level S1)
    ├── research operations
    ├── coordination and current control
    ├── independent evidence checks
    └── local intelligence and policy
```

At the product level, the two organizations need coordination over shared models, compute, and release commitments. Product S3 allocates portfolio resources; product S4 relates to the market and longer-term technology environment; product S5 owns product identity.

Inside the coding organization, delivery cells may be local S1 units and coding leadership provides the local metasystem. Its autonomy is real but bounded: an identity-changing product decision escalates to the parent level. Temporary worker spawning inside a delivery cell is task decomposition unless the worker becomes a durable operation with its own environment, autonomy, and viable relationships.
